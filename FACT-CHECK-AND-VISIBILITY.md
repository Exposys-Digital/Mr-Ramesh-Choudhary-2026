## 4. About Wikipedia (please read)

A website cannot get someone onto Wikipedia. Wikipedia's notability rule for people (WP:BIO / WP:GNG) requires **significant coverage in multiple reliable, independent, secondary sources**. Today the coverage is:

- MyNation "India Untold" profile: a promotional-style success story, probably weak.
- TradeFlock "10 Best Leaders" list: a nomination/advertising-funded list, which Wikipedia editors generally treat as not independent.
- Crunchbase, LinkedIn, the company website and this site: self-published, so they don't count.

An article created now would very likely be **deleted**. Also, writing or paying for an article about your own client is a conflict of interest, and Wikipedia requires disclosure (WP:COI / WP:PAID). Undisclosed paid editing breaks Wikipedia's terms of use.

What actually moves the needle:
1. Earn real editorial coverage, for example a feature in the Pune edition of the Times of India, Lokmat, Sakal, Pune Mirror, Indian Express Pune, the Economic Times retail section, or YourStory. Journalists cover stories with a hook: the 2001→₹300 Cr growth, the honesty/tax stance, the school.
2. Win awards from recognised bodies (industry associations, brand partners with press releases).
3. Once 3 or more independent articles exist, request an article via **Wikipedia:Articles for creation** with a COI disclosure, and let a volunteer decide.

## 5. Google Knowledge Panel – practical steps

These are in your control:
1. **Deploy this site on a domain he owns** (e.g. rameshchoudhary.in). A custom domain is a stronger entity signal than *.vercel.app. Update the `canonical`, `og:url`, JSON-LD `@id`/`url`, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` to match.
2. **Google Search Console**: verify the domain and submit `sitemap.xml`.
3. **Wikidata**: create an item for *Surya Electronics* (company, CIN, founded 2001, official website), and one for Ramesh Choudhary only if it can cite the registry and the news article. Wikidata's bar is lower than Wikipedia's, but items with no references get deleted. Disclose your connection on the item's talk page. Once an item exists, add its URL to the JSON-LD `sameAs`.
4. **Make every profile say the same thing**: LinkedIn, Crunchbase, the company website "About" page, Facebook, and a Google Business Profile for each store. Use the same name spelling ("Ramesh Choudhary"), title, founding year and a link back to this site.
5. **Link this site from suryaelectronics.in** (About / Founder page) and from his LinkedIn "Website" field.
6. **Add a real photograph** (high-res headshot, licensed for use) as `/ramesh-choudhary.jpg`, and point the JSON-LD `image` to it. Panels usually show a photo.
7. Once a panel appears, he can **claim it** ("Claim this knowledge panel", verified via Search Console or his official profiles) and suggest edits.
