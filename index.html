The journey

Nine chapters, one stubborn idea: keep going.

01

Early life

Nadol, Rajasthan

Ramesh Choudhary was born into a farming family in Nadol, in the Pali district of Rajasthan.[6] Before school he herded the family's cattle, and because the village lacked facilities he walked about five kilometres to attend class.[2][3] He credits his grandfather, a freedom fighter, and his parents' lessons in hard work for the values that guide him.[6]

02

Hardship

Too much, too young

He has recounted being married at ten, while still in Class 5, and losing his first wife soon after. By fourteen he had fallen into tobacco and alcohol. His father sent him to a hostel attached to a Jain school, where the discipline and a new sense of spirituality helped him give up both.[2][3]

03

Struggle

Fifty kilometres a day

He cleared Class 10 and Class 12, by his own account with the help of grace marks in English. He then worked in a small shop for meals rather than wages, learning to repair electronics, and cycled more than 50 km a day carrying electrical goods to sell.[2][6] At 17 he moved to Pune, where he worked as a cleaner, a shop assistant and an electronics technician.[7]

04

The spark

₹1,500 a month

Guided by a relative, he joined Pawan Electronics as a helper on ₹1,500 a month. Sales he handled are reported to have grown from a handful of units to 150–200 units a month, which convinced him he could run a shop of his own.[2]

05

2001 · Foundation

Two hundred square feet

With capital lent by his father-in-law, later repaid, he opened Surya Electronics in Pune in 2001: a 200 sq ft shop with two helpers. Turnover in the first year is reported at ₹1 crore.[2][3]

06

2008 · Resilience

Credit in a crisis

When the 2008 financial crisis squeezed household budgets, Surya partnered with Bajaj Finance to offer customers EMI (instalment) options, keeping appliances within reach of middle-class families.[2]

07

2017 · Institution

From shop to company

On 28 December 2017 the business was incorporated as a private limited company in Pune, now named Mr Surya Electronics India Private Limited (CIN U74999PN2017PTC174042), with Ramesh Choudhary as a director.[4] He has said it took fifteen years to lay a robust foundation.[3]

08

Giving back

Nation first

Through the MR Surya Social Foundation, he supports education, scholarships, healthcare, sports for underprivileged youth, yoga and tree-plantation drives.[6][7] He describes his approach as “Nation First”, promoting Indian-made products in line with Make in India, and speaks to young people about discipline and self-reliance.[6][7]

09

Today

A Pune institution

Surya Electronics was reported in 2024 to have a turnover of about ₹300 crore.[2] It operates thirteen showrooms across Pune and Satara districts[8] with more than 300 staff on roughly one lakh square feet of shop floor, and aims to move from a state-level presence towards national reach.[3] Having missed higher education himself, he supported his wife through an Executive MBA at IIM Ahmedabad,[2] and has set up a school in his grandfather's name.[3]

The company

Surya Electronics

What it is

A consumer-electronics and home-appliance retail chain headquartered in Chinchwad, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune. It sells across store and online channels and offers financing and after-sales service.[1][4]
